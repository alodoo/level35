      How it works

      Points

      • Each habit earns points based on how hard it is, and CORE habits count double. Your fullest day is worth 100 XP.
      • Lighter days, like weekends, earn less but still count for your streak.
      • Habits with a number give partial points. For example, drink 4 of your 8 glasses of water and you get half that habit’s points.

      Days

      • Solid day: all your CORE habits for that day, however many you have. Your streak grows.
      • Good day: at least 75% of your CORE. Your streak holds.
      • Perfect day: every habit ticked. You need at least 3 habits that day, with at least 2 of them CORE. It adds +1 to your perfect days, which never go down, and gives ×1.25 XP the next day.
      • Rest day: no CORE habits due. Your streak isn’t affected.

      Missing days

      • 1 missed day: your streak survives, with a warning.
      • 2 in a row: your streak resets.
      • 3 in a row: you drop a level and earn ×0.75 XP until your next solid day.

      Levels

      • Close the day to bank its XP. You can level up at most once a day, and extra XP carries over.
      • If you forget to close a day, the app asks you the next day.
      • Each level costs 100 XP up to LV 10. The cost then grows: 200 by LV 20, 400 by LV 30, and 650 for LV 35, the final boss.

      Boosts they multiply together, up to ×2

      • Streak: ×1.25 from 3 days, ×1.5 from 7 days.
      • Extra Boost ⚡: ×1.5 when you do a task, ×1.25 when you clear your whole room.
      • Perfect day yesterday: ×1.25.
      • Weekly bonus: 5 solid days in a week gives +50 XP on Sunday.

      Your habits

      • Everything is on your list from day one.
      • Times and amounts step up on their own, about every 5 solid days. “Your path” shows what’s next.

      Tasks and your room

      • Tasks are the post-its on your board, and unfinished ones move to the next day.
      • Room changes clear the clutter. Finish them all to get new decor.

      Friends

      • Friends see only your name and your streak.

      Build your plan

      Start where you are, not where you want to be. Pick your habits, say where you are now and where you want to get to, and the app builds the levels for you.

      How consistent are you right now?
      This sets how fast your times and amounts step up. Starting from zero? Pick “Rarely” for smaller steps.

      Your habits

      Do all your CORE habits on a day and it’s a solid day, which grows your streak. We suggest 2–4 CORE habits: you need at least 2 for perfect days, and with too many, one slip costs you the day. Rate how hard each habit is for you right now. Harder habits earn more XP, and CORE habits count double.

      Your to-do list

      One-time things, like booking an appointment. Each one becomes a post-it on your board. Finish one and that whole day gets a ×1.5 Extra Boost ⚡. Anything you don’t finish moves to the next day.

      Optional. You can add more any time in Tasks.

      Design your room

      One-time changes to your space that make good habits easier, like leaving your gym bag by the door. Each one shows up as clutter until you do it, and your walls warm up as you go. Clear them all for a ×1.25 Extra Boost ⚡ that day and new decor.
