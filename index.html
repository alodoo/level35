      Your friend’s code

      Each day is worth up to 100 XP: 80 for your CORE habits, 20 for the rest (CORE alone is worth 100). XP levels you up when you close the day, one level at most per day. Levels cost 100 XP up to LV 10, then grow slowly up to 650 for the last one. Each habit is worth the same every day, so a light day (like a weekend) earns less, but it still counts for your streak. A solid day = all your CORE habits; a good day = at least 75% of them. Solid days grow your streak, good days keep it. Streak boost: ×1.25 from day 3, ×1.5 from day 7. Extra Boost ⚡: ×1.5 for a task, ×1.25 for clearing your whole room. Max ×2. 5 solid days in a week = +50 XP on Sunday. A perfect day (every habit ticked, at least 5 that day) adds to your Perfect days count and gives ×1.25 XP the next day. Miss 1 day and your streak survives (you’ll get a warning). Miss 2 in a row and it resets. Miss 3 in a row and you drop a level and get ×0.75 XP until your next solid day. Forgot to close a day? The app asks you the next day. Days with no CORE habit are rest days.
