Level 35 privacy

Last updated 8 November 2026.

What Level 35 keeps

  • When you sign in with Google: your email address and your Google account ID, used only to sign you in.
  • Your progress: the habits you set up, the days you ticked, your tasks and room changes, the name you choose and your day streak.

Where it is stored

On your phone, and in a database run by Supabase in the EU (Frankfurt). The app is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Who can see it

Only you. If you share your friend code, the friends who add it see your chosen name and your day streak, nothing else. Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers or used for tracking.

Deleting your data

Email alex.lozano.d@gmail.com and your account and all its data will be deleted.
